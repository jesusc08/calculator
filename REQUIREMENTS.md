## Requirements

1. The calculator shall accept and perform addition on two numerical inputs.   

2 + 5 = 7

3 + -5 = -2

2. The calculator shall accept and perform subtraction on two numerical inputs.  

9 – 3= 6

7 - -8 = 15

3. The calculator shall accept and perform multiplication on two numerical inputs.

3 \* 2 = 6

4 \* -2 = -8

4. The calculator shall accept and perform division on two numerical inputs, provided the divisor is a non-zero number.

10 / 2 = 5

5 / 0 = undefined

5. The calculator shall clearly display the resulting output of any valid arithmetic operation.
